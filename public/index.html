Our Story

Baked with Passion,
Served with Love

Founded in 2020, Shiv Bakery started as a small kitchen dream and grew into a beloved neighborhood bakery. We believe every bite should tell a story — one of quality ingredients, time-honored techniques, and genuine care.

Our master bakers wake up before dawn every day to ensure every loaf, cake, and pastry is baked to absolute perfection. We source organic flour, real butter, and seasonal fruits to create flavors that keep our customers coming back.
